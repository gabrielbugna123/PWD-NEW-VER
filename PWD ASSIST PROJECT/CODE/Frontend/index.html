STEP-BY-STEP HELP

Assistance Guide

How to Apply for a PWD ID

The following is a general guide. Contact your local Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO) for the latest requirements and process.

1

Prepare your documents

Ask your local PDAO about the current application form and supporting documents required.

2

Visit your local PDAO

Submit your application to the designated office in your city or municipality.

3

Follow the assessment process

Follow the instructions given by the authorized personnel. Additional verification may be required.

4

Wait for the application update

Ask the office about processing time and how you will be notified.

Always use official government channels for updated requirements and assistance.
